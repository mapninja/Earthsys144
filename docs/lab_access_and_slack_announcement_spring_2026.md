# Lab Access and Slack Reminder

**Subject:** Lab Access and Course Slack Reminder

Dear Students,

As we get started, here are two quick reminders that will help things go more smoothly during the first lab meetings.

## Main Update

If you are attending lab in `Y2E2-184` (access code: `839090`), please remember that the room can be busy and shared by multiple groups. You should bring your own laptop if possible, but workstation computers are available in the room. If you need to access the building after 7:00 PM, you may need after-hours building access and any required safety clearance.

We also invite you to join the course Slack space for quick questions, troubleshooting, and informal course communication. This is often the fastest place to get help from the teaching team or from classmates.

Two optional reference texts are also available if you would like additional background reading. The Bolstad textbook is available through a read-only Google Drive link, and the Tomlin textbook is available through Archive.org (links below).

## Action Items

- Join the course Slack space before your first lab session.
- Review the lab access guide if you plan to work in `Y2E2-184` or the SGC GIS Lab.
- Bring a laptop to lab if you have one available.
- Bookmark the optional textbook links if you would like extra reference material.
