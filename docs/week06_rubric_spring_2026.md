# Week 06 Rubric: Remote Sensing, Movement Modeling, Interpolation, and Planet Imagery

The canonical Week 06 rubric is maintained here:

- [Assignments & Rubrics - Week 06](../assignments_rubrics/week06_Rubric.md)

Use that rubric for Canvas setup and grading. It treats Week 06 as one 10-point assignment with four graded components:

1. Basic Remote Sensing with Google Earth Engine
2. Tobler's Hiker Function and terrain-based distance modeling
3. Sampling & Interpolation with QGIS
4. Introducing Planet.com Satellite Imagery

The optional pan-sharpening Landsat workshop is support material and is not part of the Week 06 score unless separately assigned.
