# Week 06 Rubric: Remote Sensing, Movement Modeling, and Interpolation

The canonical Week 06 rubric is maintained here:

- [Assignments & Rubrics - Week 06](../assignments_rubrics/week06_Rubric.md)

Use that rubric for Canvas setup and grading. It treats Week 06 as one 10-point assignment with three graded components:

1. Basic Remote Sensing with Google Earth Engine
2. Tobler's Hiker Function and terrain-based distance modeling
3. Sampling & Interpolation with QGIS

The optional Planet.com and pan-sharpening Landsat workshops are support material and are not part of the Week 06 score unless separately assigned.
