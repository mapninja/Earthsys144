# Week 08 Rubric: Bonus Tree Labeling Continuation

The canonical Week 08 bonus-lab rubric is maintained here:

- [Assignments & Rubrics - Week 08 Bonus Lab](../assignments_rubrics/week08_Rubric.md)

Use that rubric for Canvas setup and grading. It treats the Week 08 bonus work as extra credit for continuing the Week 07 tree-labeling lab:

1. Each additional suitable grid square earns 1 extra credit point.
2. Each bonus grid square must include both prefire and postfire labels.
3. Up to 10 extra credit points total may be earned.

These points are added to the student’s lab points total when calculating the final grade.
