# Assignments & Rubrics - Week 08 Bonus Lab

## Week 08 Assignment Overview

Week 08 is the final lab week of the course. The regular Week 08 materials are optional support labs, but the tree-labeling bonus work continues from Week 07 as extra credit.

This bonus lab does not replace the required Week 07 Lab 04 submission. Instead, it gives students a chance to keep labeling additional suitable grid squares for extra credit after the required 4 are complete.

## Bonus Lab Page

- Bonus Lab Context: [Week 07 - TURN IN BONUS LAB - Tree Labeling in QGIS](https://mapninja.github.io/Earthsys144/week07/04_TURN_IN_BONUS_LAB_tree_labeling_in_qgis.html)
- Week overview: [Week 08 - Final Lab Week](https://mapninja.github.io/Earthsys144/week08/README.html)

## What Students Should Submit

Students should continue using the Week 07 tree-labeling submission format:

1. Two separate, unzipped GeoJSON files.
2. One file for postfire labels.
3. One file for prefire labels.
4. Any additional bonus grid squares included in those same outputs.

## Rubric

Total possible bonus points: 10 extra credit points

| Criteria | What to look for | Points |
| --- | --- | ---: |
| Additional tree-labeling grid squares | Each additional suitable grid square beyond the required 4 includes both postfire and prefire labels, follows the same labeling rules as the required work, and is complete enough for review. | 1 extra credit point per additional completed grid square, up to 10 extra credit points total |

## Grading Notes

- Only count bonus grid squares that include both prefire and postfire labels.
- Do not count partially completed or unsuitable grid squares.
- Add the earned bonus points to the student’s lab points total when calculating the final grade.
- The required Week 07 Lab 04 work still has to be completed separately from this bonus credit.
