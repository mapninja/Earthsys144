# Assignments & Rubrics - Week 00

## Week 00 Assignment Overview

Week 00 is about getting your working environment ready and confirming that you can use the two main platforms we will rely on right away:

1. QGIS, for desktop GIS work and map layout.
2. Google Earth Engine, for browser-based geospatial coding and data access.

This Week 00 assignment is graded as a single 10-point assignment made up of two required turn-ins.

## Required Lab Pages

- Lab Document: [09 - TURN IN - Introduction to QGIS: Being John Snow](https://mapninja.github.io/Earthsys144/week00/09_TURN_IN_introduction_to_qgis.html)
- Lab Document: [10 - TURN IN - Logging in to Google Earth Engine](https://mapninja.github.io/Earthsys144/week00/10_TURN_IN_logging_in_to_google_earth_engine.html)
- Supporting setup page: [07 - Installing QGIS and Plugins](https://mapninja.github.io/Earthsys144/week00/07_installing_qgis_and_plugins.html)

## What Students Should Submit

Submit both of the following:

1. A **finished image export of the John Snow map layout** from the QGIS lab.
2. A **working Google Earth Engine "Get Link" URL** to a shared script opened from a sample in the Earth Engine Data Catalog.

For the Google Earth Engine submission, students must:

- **Add a comment in the script** that identifies them by name.
- **If they make any extra edits beyond the original sample script, add inline comments that clearly indicate those changes**.

## Grading Notes

- This rubric treats Week 00 as one assignment with two required deliverables.
