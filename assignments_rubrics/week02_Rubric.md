# Assignments & Rubrics - Week 02

## Week 02 Assignment Overview

Week 02 focuses on making and collecting spatial data in three different ways:

1. Completing **one** of two introductory data-creation workflows:
   - field data collection with ArcGIS Online and Field Maps
   - georeferencing a historical map in QGIS
2. Creating vector data in QGIS by digitizing points, lines, and polygons from aerial imagery.
3. Working with raster structure in Google Earth Engine through collections, images, bands, and pixels.

This Week 02 assignment is graded as a single 10-point assignment made up of three graded components: one `PICK_ONE_TO_TURN_IN` lab, the QGIS editing lab, and the Earth Engine lab.

## What Students Should Submit

Submit all of the following:

1. **One** of the following:
   - the two ArcGIS Online URLs from the Field Maps lab
   - the exported PDF map layout from the georeferencing lab
2. The exported map layout PDF from the QGIS editing lab.
3. A working Google Earth Engine "Get Link" URL for the Earth Engine lab.

For the Google Earth Engine submission, students must:

- include their name in a comment near the top of the script
- include inline comments marking any changes they made

## Rubric

Total possible points: 10

| Criteria | What to look for | Points |
| --- | --- | ---: |
| Pick-one lab completed successfully | Student successfully completed **one** of the two `PICK_ONE_TO_TURN_IN` labs. This can be either the Field Maps workflow or the georeferencing workflow, depending on which lab the student chose. | 3 |
| Trees digitized as points | Student digitized tree features as points in the QGIS editing lab. | 1 |
| Paths and rows digitized as lines | Student digitized path and row features as lines in the QGIS editing lab. | 1 |
| Structures digitized as polygons | Student digitized structures as polygons in the QGIS editing lab. | 1 |
| Map layout includes required cartographic elements | The exported QGIS map includes a title, scale, CRS, legend, student name, and date, and clearly communicates the digitized result. | 2 |
| Google Earth Engine submission works and demonstrates the required concepts | Student submitted a working Earth Engine Get Link URL. The shared script includes the student's name in a comment, marks edits with inline comments, and shows the required workflow elements: an AOI, filtering by place and date, a selected Dynamic World probability band, use of `updateMask()`, Hansen forest-loss thresholding, and RGB and CIR Sentinel-2 visualization. | 2 |

## Grading Notes

- This rubric treats Week 02 as one 10-point assignment with three graded components: one `PICK_ONE_TO_TURN_IN` lab, QGIS editing, and Google Earth Engine.
- For the `PICK_ONE_TO_TURN_IN` portion, grade the student's chosen lab only. Students should not be penalized for not submitting the other pick-one option.
- For the QGIS portion, check whether the student successfully created point, line, and polygon features and submitted the final map layout.
- For the map layout, check for the basic cartographic elements and whether the final PDF clearly documents the student's work.
- For the Earth Engine item, the script should both function and demonstrate the concepts introduced in the lab, with clear commenting suitable for review.
