# Week 08 - Final Lab Week

Week 08 is the final lab week of the course, with an emphasis on advanced models, integrated workflows, and higher-level spatial analysis. The materials are intended to help students complete more ambitious work while reviewing key methods and analysis patterns.

All of the week’s labs are marked as OPTIONAL, giving students flexibility to prioritize the most useful advanced analyses for their final projects.
